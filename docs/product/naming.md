# Working name and future public brand

November 26, 2026 · Sam's direction

LearnLoop is a working product/code name. Sam flagged existing learning products using it and accepts keeping it internally while considering a more distinctive public name later. No replacement name is selected. This does not block the personal prototype or require renaming the repository, database, source IDs or Google project.

A focused live search confirmed overlapping uses in the same category:

| Existing use | Evidence checked November 26 |
| --- | --- |
| LearnLoop AI-assisted study platform | [golearnloop.com](https://golearnloop.com/) advertises material uploads, quizzes and progress tracking. |
| LearnLoop: AI Study Tutor, GliPpiN Toys, LLC | [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glippin.learnloop) describes flashcards, quizzes and spaced reviews. |
| LearnLoop community learning platform | [learnloop.cc](https://learnloop.cc/) advertises courses and learning communities. |

These are observed naming collisions, not findings about trademark ownership or legal clearance. Do not describe LearnLoop as a unique or cleared public brand.

Before wider promotion, a subscription launch or app-store submission, shortlist distinctive names, check exact/similar web and app-store uses, domains/handles and relevant trademark records, then have Sam choose. Do not buy domains or reserve paid services without approval. The name decision is separate from whether the app improves recall.

Keep learn.allstreetlabs.com as the selected pilot address unless Sam changes it. The Google project's display name can be revisited; its project ID is immutable and may retain the working name. The later OAuth consent branding, app title, icons and support/privacy pages should clearly identify the ASL pilot and use the approved public brand when one is selected. Review provider branding requirements at that point.

A rebrand must preserve item/set/owner IDs, source provenance, saved progress and backup compatibility. Keep any necessary migration explicit. Public profiles, subscriptions and native distribution remain later candidates, not added MVP scope.
